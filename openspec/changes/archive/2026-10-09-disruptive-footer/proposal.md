Linear-Issue: JOS-191

## Why

The page ends on a single grey line of analytics small print. A recruiter who scrolls all the way down has shown the most interest any visitor can show, and at that moment the site gives them nothing to do. The owner wants that dead end replaced with a footer that is playful and memorable, and that turns that last scroll into a booked call or a message.

## What Changes

- **The footer becomes a contact surface.** `SiteFooter` is replaced by a full-width band on its own slightly lifted dark surface. It contains:
  - Mar.IA raising an index finger with a stern "come on" face
  - the headline "Hey, you're at the bottom of my interactive CV page."
  - a subline
  - one oversized, raised call-to-action ("Come on, click on this" / "Don't make me beg") that opens the cal.com scheduling page
  - a row of icon links: LinkedIn, WhatsApp, GitHub, cal.com, and email
- **The analytics disclosure stays.** It moves, unchanged, to a meta line at the bottom of the new footer.
- **Mar.IA gets new artwork, used only in the footer.** It is three transparent layers (body, upper arm, forearm with a pointing glove). The forearm wags around the elbow in a small loop, and the loop is disabled under reduced motion.
- **WhatsApp is reached through a redirect on the site.** A new route at `/go/whatsapp` sends visitors to `wa.me/<number>`. The number lives only in a server-side secret, so it never appears in the page, the bundle, `/content`, or the chatbot's retrieval index. The route is rate limited and disallowed in `robots.txt`.
- **Two new analytics contact targets.** `contact_click` gains `github` and `whatsapp`, and the admin conversions report labels them.
- **One new palette token.** `--surface-raised` gives the footer its lifted surface, with every text tint re-measured against it.
- **Copy is content-sourced.** The footer copy lives in a new `footer` block in `content/profile.yaml`, validated by the content schema.

The `#contact` section is **unchanged**. The header nav and the contact pill still target it. The footer adds a second contact surface; it does not replace the section.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `site-footer`: the footer's structure and content source, its call-to-action and icon links, the preserved analytics disclosure, the Mar.IA illustration and its wag, no-JS rendering, reduced motion, and clearance from the fixed chat trigger.
- `whatsapp-contact-redirect`: the `/go/whatsapp` route. It keeps the number server-side only, validates the number, rate limits requests, and opts out of crawling and caching.

### Modified Capabilities

- `engagement-event-tracking`: the `contact_click` channel set grows from `scheduling`/`email`/`linkedin` to include `github` and `whatsapp`.
- `admin-reports`: the conversions breakdown by contact target includes GitHub and WhatsApp.
- `site-visual-language`: the bounded palette gains a raised surface, and every text tint must clear AA on it as well as on the page background.
- `seo-metadata-and-structured-data`: the robots policy disallows `/go/` alongside the existing `/admin` exclusion.
